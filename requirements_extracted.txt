Training and Placement Management System (TPMS)
Abstract
The Training and Placement Management System (TPMS) is a web-based application
designed to streamline and digitize the complete workflow of training and placement
activities in academic institutions. This system facilitates seamless coordination among
multiple stakeholders including administrators, Training and Placement Officers (TPOs), TnP
Assistants, Principals, and students.
TPMS enables administrators to manage departments, assign roles, and monitor system-wide
placement activities. TPOs and TnP Assistants can efficiently post job openings, upload study
materials, manage student data, and oversee the application and selection process. Students
can view job opportunities based on eligibility, apply directly through the portal, and access
relevant training resources.
The system supports role-based dashboards, real-time notifications, eligibility filtering, and
comprehensive reports and analytics, making it a powerful tool for improving transparency,
efficiency, and outcomes in the placement process. By digitizing critical operations and
centralizing information, TPMS enhances institutional effectiveness and better prepares
students for industry engagement.
Objectives
 Digitize the complete training and placement workflow.
 Maintain accurate, up-to-date records of students and placement activities.
 Simplify the job application, approval, and shortlisting processes.
 Provide real-time dashboards and analytical reports to TPOs and administrators.
 Facilitate seamless communication between students, TPOs, assistants, and institutional
leadership.
User Roles and Authentication
Role Responsibilities
Admin - Add/edit/delete TnP Officer, TnP Assistants, Principal
- Manage departments
- Assign departments to TnP Assistants
Principal - Monitor the overall TnP system and activities
- View reports and placement statistics
TnP Officer - Manage Companies
- Post job openings
- Post selection process updates
- Upload study materials (PDFs, videos, links)
- View student applications
- Reports
TnP Assistant - Upload and manage department-wise student data (CSV/manual)
- Approve/reject job openings for their department
- Upload department-specific study materials (PDFs, videos, links)
- Review and verify student applications
- Post/update selection process statuses
Student - View job openings (filtered by eligibility)
- Apply for jobs

Modules & Features
Admin Panel
 Dashboard
o Count: user, department and current academic year wise students, current job
openings, placed students
o Notifications
 Add/edit/delete users (TnP Officer, Assistant, Principal)
 Manage departments
 Assign TnP assistants to departments
 View placement statistics
TnP Officer Panel
 Dashboard
o Count: user, department and current academic year wise students, current job
openings, placed/un-placed students, min/avg/max package.
o Notifications
 Post job openings with eligibility criteria (% marks, degree, etc.)
 Upload job-wise study materials (links,videos, pdfs, etc)
 View and track0020job applications
TnP Assistant Panel
 Dashboard
o Count: user, department and current academic year wise students, current job
openings, placed/un-placed students, min/avg/max package.
o Notifications
 Post/approve/reject job openings for their department
 Upload student data department-wise (Excel/CSV or manual)
 Upload department-wise job opening wise study materials (links,videos, pdfs, etc)
 View and verify job applications from students
 Post or approve selection process results.
 Access department-specific reports.
Principal Panel
 Dashboard

Training and Placement Management System (TPMS)
Student Panel
 Dashboard
o View job openings (filtered by eligibility).
o Track applied companies and application statuses.
o Receive job-related notifications and selection updates.
 Apply for eligible jobs with a single click.
 View/download study materials provided by TnP Assistants/Officers.
Reports and Analytics
 Real-time statistics and visualization for placement and training activities.
 Placement logs with:
o Company-wise participation records.
o Drive-wise and department-wise stats.
 Eligibility-based filters and reports:
o 10th Percentage
o 12th Percentage
o Graduation Percentage
o Post-Graduation Percentage (if applicable)
 Analytics on placed vs. unplaced students.
 Min/Max/Average salary reports.
Conclusion
The Training and Placement Management System (TPMS) provides a centralized and user-
friendly platform to manage all operations related to student training and placement. By automating
workflows and offering real-time insights, TPMS empowers educational institutions to make
informed decisions, improve placement outcomes, and foster stronger industry connections.
