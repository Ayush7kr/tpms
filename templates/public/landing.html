Recruiters on record

From announcement to offer letter

A drive moves through four hands. Each one sees only what they need to act on.

Placement officer

Posts the drive

Company, role, package, and the cut-offs: CGPA, 10th and 12th marks, backlogs, branches and batch.

Department coordinator

Opens it for the branch

Each branch coordinator approves the drive for their students, or holds it back.

Student

Applies in one step

The portal checks every cut-off against the student's record. If one is missed, it says which one.

Placement officer

Tracks every round

Shortlist, test, technical and HR rounds, then the offer. Students are notified at each step.

One portal, five roles

Everyone signs in at the same place and lands on their own workspace.

Students

  • See every drive you qualify for
  • Track each application round
  • Keep your resume and skills current

Placement officer

  • Post drives with cut-offs
  • Move candidates through rounds
  • Keep the recruiter directory

Department coordinators

  • Import students from Excel or CSV
  • Approve drives for the branch
  • Verify applicants' marks

Principal

  • Placement rate by branch
  • Salary bands and top recruiters
  • Export records to CSV

Administrator

  • Staff accounts and departments
  • Assign coordinators to branches
  • Full activity log
