About The System

Next-Generation Training & Placement Architecture

The Training and Placement Management System (TPMS) transforms campus recruitment from disparate paper-and-spreadsheet files into an intelligent, auditable, and automated digital ecosystem.

Designed specifically to address the complex requirements of multi-department academic institutions, TPMS guarantees strict departmental autonomy for placement coordinators while giving institutional leadership panoramic analytical oversight.

Zero Data Leakage Department assistants manage only assigned branch rosters.
Deterministic Cut-offs 10th %, 12th %, CGPA & backlogs checked programmatically.
Student Self-Service

1-click application submission, verified academic vault, and stage progress tracker.

Recruiter Network

Tiered company directory, POC management, and automated drive staging.

Bulk Ingestion

CSV & Excel import with duplicate detection and atomic rollback protection.

NIRF / NAAC Analytics

Instant generation of salary distributions, branch placement rates, and rosters.

End-to-End Workflow

How The Placement Cycle Operates

A streamlined, transparent path from student onboarding to corporate job offers.

1
Student Onboarding

Assistants import department students via CSV/Excel or individual entry with verified academic records.

2
Drive & Eligibility

TPO posts job openings with multi-metric cut-offs; department assistants review and activate for their branch.

3
1-Click Application

Eligible students submit applications with one click. Assistant checks credentials and verifies submissions.

4
Selection & Offers

Recruitment rounds progress through Aptitude, Tech, and HR. Final offers automatically update student status.
